📖 Portal User Manual & Help Center

Welcome to the complete guide for the Pinnate Consulting Client Portal. This manual covers everything you need to know — from signing in for the first time to finding, printing, and restoring your saved reports. If you ever feel lost, this is your starting point.

🔐 Getting Access to the Portal

Access to this portal is not open to the public. It requires an active subscription with Pinnate Consulting LLC. Here is how to get started:

1
Go to the portal login page and click the "Request Access" tab.
2
Fill in your full name, company name, business email, phone number, and the reason you need access.
3
Click "Submit Access Request." Your request is immediately sent to Pinnate Consulting's dedicated approver.
4
The approver will verify your company's subscription status and respond same day to next business day with your User ID and password.
5
Use your issued credentials to sign in on the "Sign In" tab.
💡
If you have not received credentials within one business day, contact Pinnate Consulting at gamal@pinnateconsulting.com or call +1 281 796 2525.

📱 The Applications Dashboard — What Each Button Does

After signing in, you will land on the Applications Dashboard. You will see 8 application tiles. Each tile represents a dedicated business tool built specifically for your company's subscription with Pinnate Consulting.

  • Click any tile to open that application in your session. The application opens in the same window.
  • Each application is designed for a specific task (names will be updated as your subscription grows).
  • Your data inside each application is linked to your User ID — other users in your company cannot see your private inputs unless the application is designed for shared team use.
  • Application source files are fully hidden from all users — only the Pinnate Consulting administrator can access them.

💾 Saving Your Work

Every application has a "Save Report" button inside it. Here is exactly what happens when you save:

1
You complete your work inside any application (entering data, running a report, or filling out a form).
2
Click "Save Report" inside that application. You will be prompted to give the report a name (e.g., "Q1 Billing Report – April 2025").
3
The report is automatically stored in The Storage Facility under your company's folder, tagged with the application name, your User ID, and the exact date and time.
4
A confirmation message appears: "Report saved to Storage Facility."
💡
You do not need to choose a folder or file location — it is handled automatically. Think of it as saving to a cloud folder that is always organized for you.

🗄 The Storage Facility — Finding & Restoring Reports

The Storage Facility (accessible from the top navigation bar) is the central place where all your saved reports live. Think of it as your cloud filing cabinet — organized exactly like folders and sub-folders on your computer, but always available from anywhere.

  • Folders at the top represent each Application. Click a folder to filter the report list to only that application.
  • The address bar (the green path bar) always shows you exactly where you are: Portal › Your Company › Application Name.
  • Search: Type any word in the search box — report name, date, or type — to find a specific report instantly.
  • Filter by date: Use the date filter buttons (Today / This Week / This Month / All Dates) to narrow results.
  • Restore a previous report: Click the Restore button next to any report to reload it inside its original application, exactly as it was when saved.
  • Print a report: Click the Print button to open the print dialog for that report — you can print to paper or save as a PDF.
💡
Storage is organized by your company name automatically. You will never mix your data with another company's reports — each subscriber has a private, isolated storage space.

🖨 Viewing & Printing Previous Reports

1
Go to The Storage Facility from the navigation bar at the top.
2
Find the report you want — use the search box or date filters to locate it quickly.
3
Click Restore to re-open the report inside the application, or click Print to print it directly without reopening.
4
In the print dialog, choose your printer — or select "Save as PDF" to keep a digital copy on your computer.

🔑 Your Account — Password & Security Tips

  • Your User ID was assigned by Pinnate Consulting when your access was approved — it cannot be changed by you.
  • Your password was set by the administrator when your account was created. If you need to reset it, contact gamal@pinnateconsulting.com.
  • Never share your credentials with anyone — each user should have their own account.
  • Always click Sign Out when you finish your session, especially on shared computers.
